Returns and cancellations

Changed your mind?

You have the right to cancel your order within 14 days, without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day after your stickers are delivered.

To cancel, tell us before the 14 days run out. You can email , or use the cancellation form below. You do not have to use the form.

If you cancel, send the stickers back unused and in their original condition. We will email you the address to send them to. Post them within 14 days of telling us you are cancelling. You pay the cost of returning them.

We will refund the price of the stickers and the original £1.00 postage. We will pay the refund within 14 days of getting the stickers back, or of you showing us proof that you have posted them, whichever is sooner. Refunds go back to the card you paid with. We may reduce a refund if a returned sticker has been used, peeled or damaged.

Faulty, damaged or wrong item?

If a sticker arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, or we send the wrong one, email us within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer, and you will not need to send the sticker back. This is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
